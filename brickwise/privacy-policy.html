Brickwise – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the Brickwise mobile game ("the app") uses and why. The app is published by the developer named on its Google Play page ("we").

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. Your game progress (levels, stars, daily streak and settings) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • the device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location (from your IP address),
  • how you interact with ads and the app,
  • diagnostic information such as crash logs.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings, then Privacy & ad choices.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings, then Google, then Ads).
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all game data saved on your device.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Ads are limited to content rated PG (parental guidance) or lower.

Security

Information exchanged with Google's ad services is encrypted in transit.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
