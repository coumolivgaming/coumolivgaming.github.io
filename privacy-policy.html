Glide & Roll 3D — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how Glide & Roll 3D ("the game"), published by Coumoliv Gaming ("we", "us"), handles information. Contact: coumolivgaming@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect. We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or any account. The game has no sign-in. Your progress, stars, hints and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us, and they are erased when you uninstall the game or choose "Reset progress".

2. Advertising (Google AdMob). The game shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • interactions with ads and the app, such as ad views, taps and app launches;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance information;
  • basic device information, such as model, operating system version and language.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Google's use of it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Your choices.

  • Personalized ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • Advertising ID. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded videos. Watching a video for a hint is always optional.

4. Children. The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help you remove it.

5. Data sharing. We do not sell or share any personal information ourselves. The only data that leaves your device is the advertising data described in section 2, which goes to Google.

6. Security. Data sent by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

7. Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Quebec Law 25, PIPEDA or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests concern Google; see https://policies.google.com/privacy for how to exercise them. You can always contact us at coumolivgaming@gmail.com.

8. Changes. We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change, and important changes will be announced in the app or on its store page.

9. Contact. Coumoliv Gaming — coumolivgaming@gmail.com — British Columbia, Canada
