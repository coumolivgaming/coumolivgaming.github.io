Privacy Policy

Sushi Belt Rush · Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Sushi Belt Rush (the "App"), published by COUMOLIV GAMING ("we", "us"), handles, and how. We wrote it to be short and clear.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account. We do not run our own servers for the App, so we do not receive or store personal information about you.

Your game progress (levels reached, stars, settings such as sound, music, language and night mode, and a game in progress) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • interactions with ads and the App (for example, ads viewed or tapped);
  • diagnostic and crash information related to the ads service.

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your choices

  • Consent: where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App: Settings → Privacy & ad choices. If you refuse, you will see non-personalised ads.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Your data on the device: you can delete all game data by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android settings.

4. Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

5. Security and retention

Data sent to Google's advertising services is encrypted in transit. We do not keep any personal information ourselves. Google retains data according to its own policies.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act, the EU/UK GDPR, or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google through the links above. You can also contact us and we will help.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the App's store listing.
